# One job per tab (family app)

Measured with Tita Baby's sample on a 400 × 860 phone screen ("screens tall" = the whole tab, header and bottom bar included, divided by 860).

| Tab | Its one job | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ngayon** | What we eat today, and logging it. | 2.1 | **1.22** |
| **Linggo** | This week's menu and budget. | 2.8 | **1.37** |
| **Palengke** | What to buy, and how much. | 2.1 | **1.41** |
| **Pamilya** | Who eats, and how they're doing. | 2.2 | **1.17** |

"Before" is after revisions 1–4, which made Linggo taller than in the usability test (the budget box). Palengke's height depends on the shopping list; Tita Baby's has 10 items.

The main action shows without scrolling on every tab: **Kinain namin** (Ngayon), the budget line with **Baguhin** plus today's menu (Linggo), the first item to tick (Palengke), the members (Pamilya).

## Card by card

Keep = stays on the tab · Higit pa = folded under one "Higit pa" row at the bottom (stays open while the app runs once opened) · Sheet = opens from a button.

### Ngayon
| Card | Decision |
|---|---|
| Tonight's dish, cost, top benefit, ingredients, **Kinain namin**, Palitan, Iba ang kinain? | Keep (smaller pot picture; one benefit instead of two; ingredients on one swipeable line) |
| Other meals today (Almusal, Hapunan) | Keep |
| Kumusta ang pamilya | Higit pa (it's Pamilya's job) |
| Isang palit ngayong linggo | Higit pa |
| Paalala ngayong linggo | Higit pa |
| "Halimbawang araw lang ito" (sample only) | Keep |

### Linggo
| Card | Decision |
|---|---|
| Budget line: ₱ per week, "Kaya ng budget: X%" + word + bar, one-sentence gap | Keep (one compact card) |
| Budget typing box (weekly/daily, quick amounts, Hindi sigurado?, I-apply) | Sheet, from **Baguhin** |
| 7-day menu | Keep (Palitan is a small button at the end of each lunch/dinner's ingredients) |
| Balanse ngayong linggo | Higit pa |
| Karaniwang bili vs SAPAT | Higit pa |
| Halaga ng masustansyang araw (price trend) | Higit pa |
| Kalagayan sa rehiyon | Higit pa |

### Palengke
| Card | Decision |
|---|---|
| Progress (x sa y nabili, ₱ spent of budget) | Keep (smaller basket picture) |
| Shopping list | Keep ("Tantiya ⓘ" explained once at the top instead of on every row) |
| Saan ka namimili | Higit pa (the place already shows under the tab title) |
| Pinakasulit ngayong buwan | Higit pa |
| Presyo ngayong buwan (all prices) | Higit pa |
| Where prices come from (PSA note) | Higit pa |
| "No data for this place" warning | Keep (only shows when there's a problem) |

### Pamilya
| Card | Decision |
|---|---|
| Profile (switch, rename, delete) | Keep |
| Members | Keep (three to a row) |
| BNS / health-center note | Keep |
| Settings (language, halal, guide, welcome again, dark mode) | Higit pa (language and dark mode are also in the header) |
| Data sources | Higit pa |

## Before coding is final

Show `SAPAT_rev5_before_after.png` (first screen of each tab, before and after) or the live app to 1–2 people. Ask them to find: today's ulam and mark it eaten; change the budget; tick something on the list; open one family member. Note anything they look for and can't find, especially items moved into **Higit pa**.
