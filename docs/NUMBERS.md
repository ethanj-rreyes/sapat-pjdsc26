# Numbers a parent sees, by tab (family app)

Rule: plain words first; numbers only where they help, and the rest behind **Ipakita ang mga numero** or the calculator icon (**Paano kinuwenta?**).
Money is rounded to the nearest peso and percentages to whole numbers, everywhere, including the breakdowns.

Mark **Stumbled?** with what testers did in the usability test (✔ = stumbled, then note what they said). Inventory taken with Tita Baby's sample, November 2026.
